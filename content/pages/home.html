The problem

Adding products one by one doesn't scale

A new season, a supplier price list, a hundred new SEO titles: the data is already in a spreadsheet. Getting it into Shopify shouldn't take a week.

Hours of copy and paste

Title, description, price, SKU, tags, SEO, images: a dozen fields per product, typed into the Shopify admin by hand, again and again.

A strict CSV template

Shopify's own import wants its exact column names and a public web address for every image. Your supplier's sheet never looks like that.

No way back

One wrong column in a bulk import can overwrite hundreds of products, and there's no undo button. So most stores don't dare to use it.

How it works

From spreadsheet to Shopify in three steps

Load your sheet

Upload an Excel or CSV file, or paste a Google Sheet link. Click "Auto-fill from column names" and the app links your columns to Shopify fields.

Add your images

Drag in your images folder, sub-folders and all, or link a Google Drive folder. Each image finds its product by file name.

Preview, then upload

The preview shows what will change for every product, without saving anything. Happy with it? Upload, and watch the progress live.

Your sheet, your columns

Works with the sheet you already have

A supplier price list, last season's catalogue, a sheet your team keeps in Google Drive: name the columns however you like. You tell the app once which column is which, and it remembers.

  • Excel (.xlsx), CSV or a Google Sheet link, with tab choice
  • Notes above the header row? The app finds the real column names
  • Only map what you want to change: a sheet with just SKUs and prices works
  • Empty cells never erase anything in Shopify

Images

Hundreds of images, matched by name

Put the image name in a column of your sheet, then drop in the folder. Oslo Sofa.jpg, oslo-sofa_1.jpg and Oslo Sofa (2).png all go to "Oslo Sofa", in that order.

  • Upload a whole folder with sub-folders, or link a Google Drive folder
  • Add to existing images, replace them, or skip products that have some
  • Images that match no product are listed, so nothing is lost

Preview

See every change before it's made

Check image matches and preview the changes as often as you like: nothing is saved to Shopify, and previews never count towards your plan. The results show which products will be updated or created, how each was found, and what changes.

  • Filter by result, search by SKU, title or file name
  • Thumbnails of the images each product will get
  • Download the full report as CSV

Backups and undo

Changed your mind? Undo the upload

Before saving a product, the app keeps a copy of it exactly as it was. To undo an upload, preview the restore and click Restore. Only what that upload changed is put back, so later edits to price or stock are kept.

  • Restore a whole upload, or only some products
  • Skip products someone fixed by hand after the upload
  • A restore is backed up too, so it can be undone the same way

Everything in one run

Every product field, not just the basics

Variants

One row per variant with the same product key. Colour, size or any option you choose, each with its own price, SKU and barcode.

Metafields

Name a column like Material (custom.material) and it becomes a metafield. Material, care, dimensions, size guides: all from the sheet.

SEO in bulk

Meta titles, meta descriptions and image alt text for hundreds of products at once, straight from the sheet your SEO person wrote.

Tags and collections

Add tags or replace them, put products into collections by name, and create the collections that don't exist yet.

Safe test runs

Upload only the first few products, or only the SKUs you list, check them in Shopify, then run the rest. New products can start as drafts.

Runs in the background

Uploads keep going on our servers if you close the tab. Shopify's speed limits are handled for you, and products already uploaded are skipped next time.

Questions

How does the app find the right product in my store?

You choose one or more ways, in order: by SKU, barcode, handle, title, tag or Shopify product ID. The first rule that finds exactly one product is used. If none does, the row is reported as "not found", or a new product is created if you allowed that.

Will an empty cell delete something in Shopify?

No. Empty cells are skipped, and columns you don't map are never touched. Only the fields with a value in your sheet are updated.

Can I undo an upload?

Yes. Each product is backed up before it changes. In Backups, choose the upload, preview the restore and click Restore. Images the upload added are removed and replaced images come back. Backups are kept for six months.

How big can my sheet be?

Up to 50,000 rows and 25 MB per sheet, and images up to 20 MB each. Your plan sets how many products can be created or updated each month.

Does it read my customers or orders?

No. The app only asks Shopify for access to products and files. It never reads customers or orders. Uploaded sheets and images are deleted from our servers after seven days.
