How it works

From a spreadsheet to Shopify in five steps

The whole app is one page with five steps, top to bottom. Your choices are saved as you go, so next month's update is: load the new sheet, preview, upload.

Step 1

Load your product sheet

Choose an Excel or CSV file, or paste a Google Sheet link. For Google, share the sheet as Viewer with the app's address shown on screen, or set it to "Anyone with the link can view".

The first row should hold the column names. If your sheet has notes above them, leave "Row with the column names" on Auto and the app finds them. A product with variants gets one row per variant, all with the same product key.

Step 2

Map your columns

Click "Auto-fill from column names", then check the result. Two things are set here:

  • How to find each product. For example by SKU first, then by title. The first rule that finds exactly one product wins.
  • What to update. Link a sheet column to each Shopify field you want to change. Leave the rest on "Not used".
  • Variant options and metafields, such as Colour, Size or Material (custom.material).

Step 3

Add your images

Choose "Upload images" and drag in your folder (sub-folders are fine), or choose "Google Drive folder" and paste its link. Then decide what happens when a product already has images: add the new ones, replace them all, or skip that product.

Uploaded files stay on our storage for seven days, so you can run the same images again without uploading them twice.

Steps 4 and 5

Set the rules, then run

Choose whether tags are added or replaced, whether new products start as drafts or live, and whether missing products and collections may be created. Then use the three buttons in order:

  1. Check image matches: which images go to which product, and which match nothing.
  2. Preview changes: what would change for each product. Nothing is saved.
  3. Upload to Shopify: asks you once more, quoting the preview, then saves.

Image matching

How an image finds its product

The file name is compared with the "Image name" column of your sheet. Capitals, spaces, dashes and underscores don't matter; a number at the end sets the order.

Image name in the sheet: Oslo Sofa

Oslo Sofa.jpg → image 1
oslo-sofa_1.jpg → image 2
Oslo Sofa (2).png → image 3
sofas/Oslo Sofa 3.webp → image 4 (sub-folders are fine)
Oslo Sofa Grey.jpg → no match: a different name

During an upload

What happens to each product

Found

The rows with the same product key are grouped, and the product is looked up with your rules. Not found and creating is off? It's reported and skipped.

Backed up

Before anything is saved, the product is copied exactly as it is now, with a note of which fields this upload will change.

Saved

Fields, variants, metafields, collections and images are updated in Shopify. The result is added to the report, with a link to the product.

Run the same sheet again later and products that were already uploaded with the same data are skipped as "Already done". Tick "Re-send products that were already uploaded" to send them anyway.
