Pricing questions

What counts as one product?

Each product the upload creates or changes in Shopify counts once, however many variants and images it has. A product that's skipped, not found or already up to date doesn't count.

Do previews or restores use up my products?

No. Checking image matches, previewing changes, previewing a restore and restoring a backup are free on every plan.

When does my monthly count start again?

On the 1st of every month. The app shows how many products you have left this month before you upload.

What happens if I reach my limit during an upload?

The products already saved stay saved, and the rest are marked "Plan limit reached" in the report. Upgrade, or wait for next month, and run the same sheet again: the products that were already done are skipped.

How do I pay, upgrade or cancel?

All billing goes through Shopify and appears on your Shopify invoice. Open Plans in the app to upgrade or downgrade; uninstalling the app ends any paid plan.
