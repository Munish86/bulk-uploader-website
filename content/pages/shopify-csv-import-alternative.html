Use Shopify's import when…

  • You're moving a store and already have a Shopify-format export
  • Your images are already online with public links
  • You're adding new products, not changing existing ones

Use Bulk Product Uploader when…

  • Your data comes from a supplier sheet or your own spreadsheet
  • Your photos are files in a folder, named after products
  • You're updating live products and need to see, and undo, every change

The difference that matters most

Know what will change before it changes

A bulk import touches hundreds of live products at once. Bulk Product Uploader lists every product first: updated or created, how it was found, which fields change and which images it gets. Only then do you upload, and if something still looks wrong afterwards, Backups puts it back.

Questions

Can I use a Shopify product export as my sheet?

Yes. It's just another sheet: auto-fill links columns such as Title, Body (HTML), Vendor and Variant SKU, and you can find products by handle or SKU.

Do I need to host my images somewhere first?

No. Upload the folder from your computer, or share a Google Drive folder. The app uploads the images to Shopify for you.

Can I update only prices, or only SEO fields?

Yes. Map only the columns you want to change. Everything else on the product stays as it is.
