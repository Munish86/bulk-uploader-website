Features

Everything you need to manage products in bulk

Sheets

Any sheet, any column names

Excel, CSV or Google Sheets

Upload a file up to 25 MB and 50,000 rows, or paste a Google Sheet link. Choose the tab; the header row is found by itself, even under a few lines of notes.

Auto-fill the mapping

One click links columns like "Product Name", "Selling Price", "Brand" or "EAN" to the right Shopify fields. Fix anything it got wrong; your mapping is saved for next time.

A ready-made template

Starting from scratch? Download the Excel template from the app: a column for every field, a filled-in example with variants, and a short guide.

Finding products

The right product, every time

Tell the app how to recognise your products, in order. It tries each rule from top to bottom and uses the first one that finds exactly one product, so a duplicate title never updates the wrong item.

  • By SKU, barcode, handle, title, tag or Shopify product ID
  • Products that aren't in Shopify yet can be created, as drafts or live
  • Rows that match nothing are reported, never guessed

Fields

What you can create and update

Map only the columns you want to change. Empty cells are skipped, so they never erase anything in Shopify.

Running an upload

Check, preview, upload

Three buttons, always in the same order. The first two never save anything to Shopify, so you can run them as often as you like. A checklist tells you when everything is ready.

  • Test with only the first N products, or only the keys you list
  • Live progress with time left, a full log, and a Stop button
  • Products already uploaded with the same data are skipped next time
  • Speed from 1 to 8 products at a time; Shopify's limits are handled

Backups and undo

Put back exactly what an upload changed

Every product is backed up before it changes, on every plan. A restore puts back only the fields that upload touched, so a price you changed by hand last week stays as it is.

  • Fields, variants, metafields and collections set back
  • Images the upload added are removed, replaced ones come back
  • Preview a restore before running it, and undo a restore too
  • Backups kept for six months

Built to be safe

Your store and your data, treated carefully

Only products and files

The app asks Shopify for access to products and files, nothing else. It never reads customers or orders.

Files deleted after 7 days

Sheets and images you upload are removed from our storage automatically. Uninstall, and all your store's data is deleted.

Google, only what you share

For Google Sheets and Drive, the app reads only the files you share with it, and only when you start a run.
