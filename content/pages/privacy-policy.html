This policy covers two things: this website, and the Bulk Product Uploader app for Shopify ("the app"). It explains what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

This website

Forms

When you join early access or send a message, we save what you type in the form: your email address and, if you give them, your name, store address, how you add products today, how many products your store has and your message, together with the page and the time you sent it. We use it only to answer you, and for early access to email you when the app is live. We don't sell it, share it or add you to a newsletter.

To stop spam, the website counts how many forms come from one internet connection in an hour. It keeps only a scrambled code made from the connection's IP address, for one hour, never the address itself.

Cookies and tracking

The website sets no cookies for visitors and uses no advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from this website itself, not from third parties. If we add analytics later, this page will say so first.

How long we keep it

Early-access sign-ups are deleted within 12 months of the app going live. Messages are kept for as long as we need them to help you, and at most two years.

The Bulk Product Uploader app

What the app can access in Shopify

The app asks Shopify for two permissions: products (to find, create and update products, variants, collections and metafields) and files (to upload product images). It does not ask for, read or store customer or order data.

What the app stores

  • Your store's address and name, the plan you are on, and the access token Shopify gives the app when you install it.
  • Your settings: column mapping, update rules and the links you enter (Google Sheet, Google Drive folder).
  • Sheets and images you upload. They are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • A backup of each product before the app changes it, so you can undo an upload. Backups are deleted automatically after 6 months.
  • Run history: reports and logs of image checks, previews, uploads and restores, kept for 6 months.
  • The number of products the app created or updated each month, for your plan's limit.

Changes the app makes in Shopify

Only when you start an upload or a restore, and only to the products, fields and images your sheet and settings name. Checking image matches and previewing never change anything.

Google Sheets and Google Drive

If you use a Google Sheet or a Drive folder, the app reads only the files you share with its service account (or that you made public with a link), and only when you start a run. It never changes them.

Where the data is kept

On the app's servers and its file storage, used only to run the app. The data is not sold or shared with anyone else. Payments are handled by Shopify; the app never sees card details. The app runs on these service providers, which process the data only on the app's behalf: Railway (servers and database), Cloudflare R2 (uploaded files and reports) and, if you use a Google Sheet or Drive folder, Google.

Deleting your data

When you uninstall the app, it stops all runs at once. Shopify then asks the app to delete your store's data, and the app deletes all of it: settings, files, backups, reports and usage. You can also ask for deletion at any time by email.

Your rights and contact

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, from this website or the app. Write to [bu_support_email]; we answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.
